## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to Austin Mander's public site who are interested in motion systems, creative engineering, and the craft behind high-performance web interaction. They should be able to discover a study, experience it immediately, and make its core mechanism legible without leaving the page.

### Brand Personality
Editorial, rigorous, and quietly assured. The surrounding Austin Mander chrome should feel calm and precise; the individual studies are the expressive artwork and retain their own palettes and typographic voices.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the Byline system for series-level framing: warm paper, restrained rules, Fraunces and Source Serif with IBM Plex Mono furniture. Inside each study, preserve the source art direction exactly. Avoid generic product cards, glossy effects, external assets, or visual chrome that competes with the motion work.

### Design Principles

- Frame the work quietly; let each study own the room.
- Make engineering evidence visible through live numbers, not explanatory detours.
- Treat typography, spacing, and motion as one editorial system.
- Keep every experience self-contained, immediate, mobile-safe, and respectful of reduced motion.
- Prefer explicit mechanics and measurable budgets over ornamental effects.
